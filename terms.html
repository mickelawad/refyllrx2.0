Terms of Use

These terms govern use of the RefyllRx website and patient portal. They do not replace a pharmacist’s professional obligations or a patient’s statutory rights.

No automatic prescribing or treatment

Account creation, questionnaire submission, eligibility screening or payment does not guarantee a prescription, medication, dispensing, diagnosis or treatment. A regulated professional must independently determine whether a service is appropriate and within scope.

Accurate information

Patients must provide information that is accurate and complete to the best of their knowledge and promptly correct material changes that may affect care.

Pharmacy choice

Patients retain the freedom to choose their pharmacy. Selecting RefyllRx is voluntary. A patient may choose another pharmacy in the future subject to applicable legal, clinical and prescription-transfer requirements.

Virtual care and referrals

Virtual care is used only when the pharmacist determines it is appropriate. RefyllRx may require in-person assessment, additional information, identity verification or referral to another health-care provider.

Account security

Users are responsible for protecting their credentials and should not share passwords. Suspected unauthorized access should be reported promptly to the pharmacy operator.

Professional and pharmacy identification

The production service must identify the actual Ontario pharmacy operating the service, including its legal pharmacy name, physical pharmacy address, telephone number and other required operator information. Those facts are operator-supplied and are not fabricated by this website build.

Governing requirements

Use of the service remains subject to applicable Ontario and Canadian law and to the professional standards, policies and ethical obligations governing the regulated professionals providing care.
