Virtual Care Consent

Virtual care is optional. A pharmacist must determine whether virtual care is appropriate for the patient, the service and the circumstances.

Your choice

You may choose whether to receive care virtually. Choosing virtual care does not prevent you from requesting an appropriate alternative method of care where available.

Limitations

Virtual assessment can be limited by technology, the inability to perform some physical examinations, incomplete information, connectivity problems or the need to verify findings in person. A pharmacist may stop or defer a virtual assessment and recommend in-person or urgent care when clinically appropriate.

Privacy

Virtual care involves the collection and use of personal health information through electronic systems. RefyllRx will use the information for the assessment and related pharmacy-care purposes described in the Privacy Notice, subject to applicable law and safeguards.

Clinical decisions

Submitting an online questionnaire is not a prescription and does not guarantee treatment. The pharmacist remains responsible for independent professional judgment, scope-of-practice requirements, documentation, informed consent to treatment where required, and referral when appropriate.

Location

The patient must accurately state their physical location during the virtual-care encounter. This RefyllRx pathway is configured for patients physically located in Ontario; cross-jurisdictional care may require different legal and professional requirements.

Not for emergencies. Do not use the portal for an emergency or a rapidly worsening condition requiring immediate assessment.
