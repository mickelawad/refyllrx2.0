Privacy Notice

This notice explains how RefyllRx is intended to collect, use, store and disclose personal information and personal health information in an Ontario pharmacy-care context.

What we collect

Depending on the service requested, information may include identity and contact details, date of birth, address, Ontario health-card information when applicable, insurance/coverage information, prescriptions, medication history, allergies, medical history, assessment responses, clinical notes, communications, consent records and service/audit information.

Why we collect and use it

Information is collected and used only for lawful, reasonably necessary purposes connected with creating and maintaining a patient record; providing pharmacy and clinical services; verifying identity and eligibility; professional assessment; prescription fulfillment; delivery requested by the patient; communications; payment and claims processing; quality, security and audit functions; and legal or professional obligations.

Consent and patient rights

Where consent is required, it must be knowledgeable and voluntary. Patients may give or withhold consent and may withdraw consent by providing notice, subject to legal exceptions; withdrawal is not retroactive. Patients may also have rights to access and request correction of their health records and to raise a privacy concern or complaint.

Disclosure and service providers

Information may be disclosed only as permitted or required by law and only to the extent reasonably necessary. This may include health-care providers involved in care, insurers or claims processors where authorized, delivery/technology providers acting under appropriate safeguards, regulators, or others where the patient has expressly authorized disclosure or the law permits or requires it.

Safeguards and retention

RefyllRx uses authentication, role-based access controls, row-level database security and audit records. The pharmacy operator remains responsible for appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, vendor agreements, retention schedules, breach response and staff training.

Ontario and other privacy laws

Ontario personal-health-information practices are principally governed by applicable Ontario health-privacy requirements, including PHIPA. PIPEDA may apply to some commercial personal-information activities. HIPAA is a U.S. law and applies only where a RefyllRx entity or service provider is actually subject to HIPAA; this website does not represent that HIPAA governs every RefyllRx interaction.

Privacy contact

Website privacy inquiries can be sent to info@refyllrx.com. The operating pharmacy’s designated privacy contact, legal pharmacy identity and complaint process must be displayed when live pharmacy services are activated.

Operator configuration required: the final privacy notice must match the operating pharmacy’s actual vendors, data locations, retention practices and information-sharing arrangements. These operational details cannot be inferred from the website code.
