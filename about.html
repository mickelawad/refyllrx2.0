About RefyllRx

Healthcare made simpler.

RefyllRx is a digital care experience designed for Ontario patients who want a convenient way to complete health assessments, communicate securely and access pharmacy services when appropriate.

Professional review comes first

Submitting an online assessment does not guarantee a prescription or treatment. A regulated healthcare professional independently reviews the information and determines the appropriate next step.

Privacy-focused access

Patient accounts use authenticated access and protected database controls. Sensitive documents such as insurance cards are stored privately and are not served as public website assets.

Pharmacy choice

Patients retain the freedom to choose their pharmacy. Choosing RefyllRx for a requested service is voluntary.

Contact

General website inquiries can be sent to info@refyllrx.com. This website is not an emergency service.
