Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions.

Does completing an assessment guarantee a prescription?

No. A regulated professional reviews the information and independently determines whether treatment is appropriate and within scope.

Can I choose a different pharmacy?

Yes. Pharmacy choice remains with the patient, subject to applicable prescription-transfer, clinical and legal requirements.

How is my information protected?

The patient portal uses authenticated access, row-level database controls and private storage for sensitive document uploads. See the Privacy Notice.

Can I upload my insurance card?

Yes, after you sign in. Insurance-card files are limited to supported image/PDF types and stored in a private bucket with access controls.

What if my concern is urgent?

RefyllRx is not an emergency service. Use emergency services for urgent or life-threatening concerns.

Where can I read the virtual-care information?

See the Virtual Care Consent before submitting a clinical assessment.
